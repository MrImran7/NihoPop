# Vocabulary basics

Four original lessons, six words each, 12 questions each (24 words, 48 exercises).
Choose Vocabulary basics in the lesson-group picker.

| Lesson | Words |
| --- | --- |
| People | 人, 友達, 先生, 学生, 子供, 家族 |
| Food and drink | 水, お茶, ご飯, パン, 魚, 卵 |
| Places | 学校, 駅, 店, 病院, 図書館, 公園 |
| Everyday objects | 本, かばん, 電話, 鍵, 時計, 傘 |

Each introduction teaches spelling, kana reading and English meaning before practice.
The first six exercises ask Japanese → English; the next six ask English → Japanese.
Kana readings remain visible for kanji in prompts, choices and feedback. This tests
meaning recognition, not unsupported kanji recall. Kana-only words are not duplicated.

English labels identify the sense taught; they are not exhaustive dictionary entries.
Notes explain context-dependent senses (ご飯: cooked rice/meal; 時計: clock/watch),
alternative spellings and the small-ょ contrast in 病院/美容院.
Distractors come from the same topic and do not use synonyms as competing answers.

## Progress and review
New IDs begin with vocabulary-. Existing greeting/hiragana/katakana IDs and question
sets are untouched. All lesson best scores remain independent. Review mistakes uses
the shared engine and never replaces the saved lesson score. Queues are session-only.

## Scope and references
This milestone introduces everyday nouns. It is not a complete JLPT word list and
does not teach grammar, listening, pitch accent, handwriting or typed recall.
Reference resources for readings and everyday word senses are the Japan Foundation's
Irodori vocabulary lists:
- [Starter word list](https://www.irodori.jpf.go.jp/assets/data/wordlist_X.pdf)
- [Elementary vocabulary resources](https://www.irodori.jpf.go.jp/en/resources.html)

Lesson selection, wording and exercises are original. No source audio, illustrations
or curriculum pages are bundled.

## Automated checks
All four lessons run in both directions, with independent old/new scores. Objects
also runs with enlarged text. Tests cover picker labels, review with kana readings,
reopening the progress store, and all 24 word/reading/meaning mappings.
Actual device persistence, fonts and TalkBack remain physical checks.
