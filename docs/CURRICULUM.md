# Hiragana reading course

## Coverage
The catalog has 26 lessons including greetings. The hiragana sequence covers:
- 46 basic characters, including を and ん.
- 25 dakuten/handakuten forms, including ぢ and づ.
- 33 common yoon combinations and the rare ぢゃ/ぢゅ/ぢょ forms.
- Small っ versus full-sized つ, long vowels, は/へ/を as particles, and a reading checkpoint.

Lessons vary in length: three-character groups have six exercises, five-character
groups ten, and paired combination groups twelve. Reading-rule lessons have six.
Each recognition lesson practices both kana-to-romaji and romaji-to-kana.
The original seven lesson IDs and exercises remain stable so saved scores retain meaning.

## Conventions and limits
Use shi, chi, tsu and fu. を is labelled wo in its character lesson to distinguish
the spelling from お, with an explicit note that the object particle is pronounced o.
ぢ/づ usually share the standard pronunciations of じ/ず. Reverse questions are
scoped to the current lesson, and never offer both same-sounding spellings as alternatives.
Rare d-row combinations are explicitly labelled; the usual ja/ju/jo spellings are taught separately.
Long-vowel exercises retain kana-based spellings ei/ou in romaji and explain their
usual long-vowel pronunciation. They do not imply that every adjacent vowel pair
in Japanese is a single long vowel.

This is a complete modern core *reading* course, not a pronunciation or handwriting
qualification. Historical ゐ/ゑ, specialist extensions such as ゔ and small vowel
loanword spellings, and handwritten stroke order are outside this milestone.
No audio or writing assessment is present. Completion means finishing the exercise
set, not demonstrated mastery.

## Content review references
Mappings and core reading rules were checked against The Japan Foundation:
- [Irodori kana and reading reference](https://www.irodori.jpf.go.jp/assets/data/Kana_all.pdf)

Explanations and question choices are written for NihoPop. No source illustrations,
audio, mnemonic text or curriculum pages are bundled.
