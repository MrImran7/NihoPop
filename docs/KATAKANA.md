# Katakana reading course

27 lessons, 260 exercises. Katakana lessons have separate IDs starting with
katakana-; no existing hiragana IDs or exercise sets are changed.

## Coverage
- 46 basic katakana, including ヲ and ン.
- 25 voiced/semi-voiced forms.
- 33 common combinations plus the three explicitly labelled rare ヂ forms.
- 12 selected loanword combinations: ファ/フィ/フェ/フォ,
  ティ/ディ/トゥ/ドゥ, シェ/ジェ/チェ/イェ.
- Similar-shape recognition (シ/ツ, ソ/ン, ク/ケ).
- ー for vowel length, small ッ for consonant length, and word-reading practice.

The five new groups are Katakana basics, Katakana voiced, Katakana combinations,
Katakana loanwords and Katakana reading rules. Existing hiragana groups remain.

## Conventions
Romaji uses shi, chi, tsu and fu. ヲ is labelled wo to distinguish its spelling,
with an explicit note that it is usually pronounced o. ヂ/ヅ and rare ヂ combinations
are explained as alternative spellings with shared standard readings; reverse
questions are scoped to the lesson and never pit synonymous spellings against each other.
Reading exercises represent ー with doubled vowels (aa/ii/uu/ee/oo).
Loanword readings are distinct from IME keyboard sequences.

This is reading and recognition, not an audio or handwriting course. Loanword
extensions are a selected introduction, not an exhaustive inventory. Historical
ヰ/ヱ, additional extensions such as ヴ forms, and stroke-order instruction are outside scope.

## Review and persistence
Lesson results use the existing local store and mistake-review flow. Review scores
never overwrite a lesson best. Review queues last only for the current session.
Tests seed existing hiragana results and confirm every new lesson preserves them.

## References
Core mappings and reading rules were checked against The Japan Foundation's
[Irodori kana reference](https://www.irodori.jpf.go.jp/assets/data/Kana_all.pdf),
katakana pages 4–6. Explanations, questions and distractors are original; no source
artwork, audio or pages are bundled.
